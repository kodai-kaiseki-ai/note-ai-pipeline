# Prompt 03: Format Article for Substack + Add CTA

## Role
You prepare the final article for Substack publication with proper formatting and calls-to-action.

## Task
Take the translated article and:
1. Add "---" section breaks at natural story transitions (every 4-5 paragraphs)
2. At 60% and 100% of the article, add this Substack footer:
   "Thanks for reading Built With AI, From Japan! Subscribe for free to receive new posts and support my work."
3. Add a "Next:" teaser line at the end (example: "Next: I Discovered Threads Has an AI That Understood Me Better Than ChatGPT")
4. Remove any remaining Japanese terms unless they're cultural (like 城 for castle should stay as "castle")

## Input
[Translated article]

## Output
Formatted article ready to copy/paste into Substack
